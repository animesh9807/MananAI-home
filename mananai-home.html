Manan AI

Turning static lectures into interactive classrooms.

A low-latency teaching platform for real classrooms. Lecture slides, a live adjustable physics simulation, and AI-generated summaries and quizzes, together on one smartboard — so a class can see what's being taught, not just hear about it.

The problem, and the fix

The problem

Lectures built on static slides and passive PDFs make abstract ideas hard to picture — like how a launch angle changes a projectile's path. And a teacher has no real sense of whether the room is following along until the test comes back.

What Manan AI does

One screen, one class: the lecture material, a live simulation the teacher can adjust in real time, AI notes that track the current topic, and short quizzes the whole room answers together — no laptops or extra devices needed.

How it works
1

Upload

The teacher adds their lecture PDF to the dashboard — stored locally, no setup required.

2

Present

The smartboard shows a live simulation on one side, AI-generated notes on the other.

3

Check in

Short quizzes surface how well the class is following, right in the moment.
